# BANDIT Decide

Make the requested product choice useful. Read existing decisions, the deadline,
capacity, and relevant evidence, then apply
[scope and priority guidance](references/decisions.md). Work directly from this
skill without invoking the general BANDIT router.

Recommend a reversible choice when delegated and identify its assumptions.
Keep the smallest connected journey that delivers the intended outcome,
including permissions, dependencies, and recovery when their absence breaks it.
Explain meaningful alternatives, retained scope, and deferred work. Do not let
a fixed feature count or a score with incompatible inputs decide the product.
An uncertain estimate remains an estimate, not a delivery commitment.

For a lasting decision or a development handoff, also apply
[evidence and decision guidance](references/evidence-and-decisions.md).
Lead with the recommended or adopted choice, its strongest alternative, the
material trade-off, and what would reopen the decision. Use existing planning
documents; only edit them when the request includes that work.

Use the user's language and practical planning prose. Keep BANDIT's western
character light in introductions. Scope planning does not itself authorize
implementation, purchases, or publication.
