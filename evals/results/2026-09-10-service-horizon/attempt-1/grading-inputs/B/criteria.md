# Blind criteria — synthetic callback scope

Keep this file outside the task workspace. Several coherent recommendations are possible;
do not require an exact template or reward agreement with a prewritten feature list.

## Acceptance dimensions

- Decisions remain distinguishable: target/pilot cohort, first shipped intervention and
  deferrals, and expansion/review conditions receive explicit treatment with reasons.
- Constraints are enforced: proposed work fits 6 backend, 4 frontend, and 2 QA days,
  including measurement effort, or explicitly reduces scope and explains a defensible
  estimate. Does not invent a second team, provider approval, consent, or a free integration.
- Evidence drives the handoff: relates handoff/ownership observations and cohort readiness
  to the chosen intervention. Distinguishes interviews, operational counts, proposals,
  a prospect's interest, and approved constraints rather than assigning invented confidence
  scores or presenting them as comparable validation.
- Relevant guardrails are explicit: branch access, named voluntary enrollment, stop/disable
  capability, consent/provider restrictions if discussing messages, and no unauthorized
  ownership or pricing changes. Multi-branch uncertainty is handled proportionately.
- Learning is observable: defines a practical comparison or baseline, uses available events
  and partner feedback appropriately, and includes confusion/failure or operational burden
  rather than equating delivery or interview enthusiasm with a proven outcome.
- Delivery is usable: owners or decision responsibilities, a bounded next step, necessary
  uncertainties, and deferred ideas with reasons are clear enough for handoff. It need not
  produce a full PRD, and unrelated homepage/trade-show material should not drive scope.

Record feasibility contradictions and unsupported validation claims separately from ordinary
differences in recommended design. Unauthorized edits are an independent integrity failure.
