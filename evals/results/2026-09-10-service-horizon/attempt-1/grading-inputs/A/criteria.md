# Development rubric

Defined before the recorded runs. These synthetic cases target design concerns
used to build PM Craft, so passing them is development evidence, not an unbiased
general PM benchmark. Grade meaning from the full output, not keywords or headings.
Each condition is pass, partial, or fail, with a supporting excerpt or locator.
Record violations even if an output is otherwise useful.

| Case | Observable conditions |
| --- | --- |
| 01 multiple decisions | (1) Response belongs to both a participant and a particular decision, so lodging/date do not overwrite each other. (2) Joining and link viewing differ; proposes workable no-login identity and a duplicate/rename limitation. (3) Replacement before close changes one contribution; closed/final states have explicit authority, tie/nonresponse handling, and no silent stale update. (4) Complete small scope plus observable acceptance criteria; draft policy and unexecuted validation labeled. |
| 02 offer change | (1) Removes current payment/checkout/revenue assumptions and creator-only editing while preserving designated customer decision authority. (2) Preserves historical eight payments/two refunds and distinguishes actual net collections from hypothetical revenue. (3) Avoids causal price claims across simultaneous product/audience changes; separates 4/6 repeat opportunity rate from 4/14 senders and 4/24 registrants. (4) Retains still-relevant old approval evidence with its v1 scope; marks checkout historical and new shared-edit checks unexecuted without claiming software concurrency validation. |
| 03 intent review | (1) Uses designated adopted policy despite a newer generated PRD. (2) Flags automatic approval on link open and acceptance carried across changed amount, with policy/code locators. (3) Distinguishes document/code consistency from independent fidelity; existing test supports wrong rule and does not prove current execution. (4) Leaves source unchanged and does not execute tests or assert observed runtime failure. |
| 04 incomparable scores | (1) Does not divide by zero or silently impute missing inputs. (2) Does not equate sessions/month to users/quarter or subjective 8/10 to an evidenced 80%. (3) Gives a useful provisional priority tied to the core task and states what is needed for a valid quantitative comparison. (4) Honors the narrow request without stalling, invented data, or a full strategy workshop. |
| 05 small research | (1) Chooses a provisional segment and one feasible cheap test. (2) Distinguishes hypothesis/target from observations, interest from payment or repeat behavior. (3) Proceeds without unnecessary questions, external search, or files; keeps the request small. (4) Explains what observation would inform the next decision without invented validation or a universal cutoff. |

For a paired run use the same request, fixtures, host, and output cap. Supply the
appropriate upstream workflow and referenced skills, not a bare isolated file
when its documented command supplies essential behavior. Preserve raw outputs.
Do not infer population-level superiority from these few development cases.
