## Independent arithmetic

The raw inputs in both cases specify a 3.3% collection fee, 2,000 won per store-month, 30 support minutes at 25,000 won/hour, 60,000 won fixed monthly cost, and an additional one-time 40-minute onboarding (`input/costs-and-delivery.md:9` through `:14`). Thus:

- Support = 12,500 won per store-month; onboarding = 16,666.6667 won per new store.
- Recurring contribution = monthly fee × 0.967 − 2,000 − 12,500.
- Five-store first-month result = 5 × (recurring contribution + setup fee × 0.967 − 16,666.6667) − 60,000.

## Concrete remaining gaps and assessment limits

1. **C's measurement wording could be more precise.** `C/output.md:15` labels the first two rates as opportunities but calls the denominator of 20/28 “생성된 메모 기준.” The raw unit is a memo-present handoff opportunity, not an individual memo (`C/input/research-records.md:22`). The adjacent labeled counts and correct totals support the intended interpretation, so this is a clarification, not a demonstrated denominator error. Use “메모가 있었던 인계 기회 28회 중 읽음 표시 20회” in the operating record and define the same unit for `C/output.md:73`.

2. **D's final usage rule has modest operational ambiguity.** `D/output.md:79` defines opportunity-based counts, while `:80` says “두 주 모두 사용” and a 70% read rate without spelling out the weekly minimum observation count. Define what constitutes use in each week and retain an explicit inconclusive state for insufficient observations. `:82` already provides that state; the omission does not negate the independent, fully specified purchase experiment at `:77` or fail J12-3.

3. **D should assign its storage outlay to a cash envelope.** The economics retain 2,000 won per store (`D/output.md:119`, `:123`), but `:125` does not separately allocate the maximum 10,000 won cash outlay. Its 320,000 won unallocated reserve readily covers this; even adding 10,000 produces only 490,000 of the 800,000 cap. This is an allocation clarification, not a demonstrated overspend or deleted economic cost. C already includes a 50,000 won store-variable-cost envelope (`C/output.md:111`).

4. **Both economic results remain baseline estimates rather than full realized trial cost.** The plans reserve support availability and closeout time (`C/output.md:43`, `:51`; `D/output.md:37`, `:38`, `:51`) while calculations use the supplied 30-minute support assumption. Availability is not necessarily additional exclusive labor, and no actual time record exists here; it would be unjustified to automatically price every open window as separate consumed support. Nevertheless, the eventual log should distinguish actual inquiry, reminder, recovery, and closeout minutes from onboarding, and state whether closeout is included in that support estimate. If two reserved closeout hours become additional labor beyond the modeled support, they add 50,000 won and change the five-store short-offer estimate to **−195,726**. The outputs already call the trials loss-making learning activities and provide time tracking (`C/output.md:74`, `:109`, `:113`; `D/output.md:73`, `:121`, `:123`); no whole-business profitability claim is established.

5. **Execution proof is outside this assessment.** `C/output.md:113` claims JavaScript calculation and `D/output.md:119` claims tool-calculated values. Independent recomputation confirms the numbers, but the claimed tool use itself was not inspected. Likewise, the proposed role separation, manual fallback, support response times, and closeout procedure have not been implemented or exercised. That missing execution proof is not a content failure for this answer-only task; the report makes no finding about workspace integrity or external actions.
