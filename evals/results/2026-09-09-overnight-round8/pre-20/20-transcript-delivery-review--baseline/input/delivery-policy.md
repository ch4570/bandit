# Studio delivery and source-file policy

Synthetic fixture. Document copy exported 2026-09-07 from the studio's decision
register. This export date is not a new policy decision.

## D1 — adopted 2026-07-30

Owners: studio producer Leah and operations lead Omar. Applies to TranscriptDock
recordings in this studio, including the next production release.

A provider result is processing output. Delivery requires a transcript saved in
the studio's storage and a confirmed successful export by its assigned editor.
The recording page must distinguish processing, available for export, and
delivered. An export confirms receipt of a file, not transcript quality approval.

Source audio remains available until the assigned editor explicitly releases
it after that export. Release is a separate action, not inferred from provider
completion or download. Keep transcript downloads for 30 days. Reuse current
studio membership and ownership; another studio must not gain recording access.

## D2 — adopted; approval date not supplied in this export

Owners: Leah and Omar. The register marks this rule adopted, not proposed. It
does not include a dated approval entry for D2.

Preserve every submission attempt under its recording. There may be only one
unresolved provider attempt for a recording at a time. A staff member may retry
after reconciling the existing attempt's outcome; a timeout alone does not
authorize another submission. The current manual procedure uses the provider's
status lookup when an attempt ID is available. Recovery when no ID was returned
still needs an operating decision before unattended retries can be enabled.
