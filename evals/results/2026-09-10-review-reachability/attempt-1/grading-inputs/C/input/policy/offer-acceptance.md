# Synthetic adopted policy: CedarRooms offer responses

Approved by the operations committee on 2026-09-16 for the invented CedarRooms cooperative.
This policy supersedes the earlier informal practice of confirming changes over chat.

A reservation offer identifies a studio, date and time, room configuration, and attendance
limit. Each published revision has an immutable revision ID. A change to any of those
fields produces a new published revision, even if the reservation ID stays the same.

Only the reservation's assigned organizer may accept its current published revision.
An active workspace membership is necessary but does not make every member the organizer.
Workspace managers may publish offers and inspect their workspace's reservations; they do
not accept on the organizer's behalf during this pilot.

An acceptance records agreement to the specific revision shown to that organizer. Repeating
the same organizer's acceptance of the same reservation and revision is one logical action,
including after a lost response. A new revision requires the organizer to review and accept
its changed contents; an earlier acceptance does not carry forward. A request naming a
superseded revision must explain that the offer changed and must not accept it.

Recording an acceptance and securing the room are separate states. The room ledger is
authoritative for allocation. Members must be able to distinguish “Response received” from
“Room secured” until the ledger applies the acceptance.

The committee has not decided what should happen when an organizer withdraws a response
while its acceptance is awaiting ledger application. Operations owns that pending decision;
this policy does not establish a winner or authorize an automatic allocation release.
