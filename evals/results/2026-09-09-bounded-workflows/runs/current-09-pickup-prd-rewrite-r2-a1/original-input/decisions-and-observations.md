# Synthetic decisions and observations

## Historical record

- 2026-08-14: Seven of ten existing members in a staff-assisted paper exercise said they
  would like to choose a confirmed pickup slot. Staff manually checked availability.
  No unattended reservation flow, signup flow, or payment was tested.
- 2026-08-20: The planning group selected an instant-confirmation concept, conditional on
  a partner inventory API and refundable-deposit integration. Neither integration was tested.
- 2026-09-04: In six observed phone requests at West, staff approved four and declined two
  because the requested tools were already out. This was an observation of the phone service,
  not a test of the new app or a measured improvement over a baseline.

## Current pilot decision, 2026-09-10

The partner API will not be available for this pilot. The board has removed deposits from
the pilot. Use requests followed by staff approval, with no payment collection, authorization,
refund, or automatic confirmation. Begin with invited, already-verified members only.

The pilot has 10 named tool types and two staffed pickup depots, East and West. Members
choose a depot and submit one tool request at a time. Staff check the local ledger before
approving or declining. Display “Requested — awaiting staff approval” on successful submission;
submitting a request does not reserve stock or promise a pickup slot.

An approved request receives a pickup window chosen by staff. Members may withdraw a request
while it is awaiting approval. Cancellation after approval is handled by depot staff by phone
during this pilot. There is no automatic 48-hour cancellation policy: the board has asked
operators to record uncollected items while it decides a longer-term policy.

We want to learn whether members and staff can complete this request-and-approval workflow
without confusing a request with a confirmation. No adoption target, conversion uplift, or
revenue result has been validated. Review the pilot after two staffed weeks before expanding
the tool list, opening public signup, or adding unattended pickup.
