# Synthetic cooperative calendar

The autumn repair fair is planned for October. Volunteers are collecting suggestions for
tool-maintenance workshops and comparing a squirrel illustration with a toolbox illustration
for printed posters. The website's existing green palette remains usable for the pilot.

The public newsletter has a separate mailing list from verified member accounts. A proposed
newsletter signup form has no approved delivery date. The partner lending library has asked
to exchange posters; it is not sharing member or inventory data with BorrowBox.
