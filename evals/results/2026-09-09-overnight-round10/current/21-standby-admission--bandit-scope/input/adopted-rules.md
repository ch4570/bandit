# Standby decision record

Mara adopted these rules on October 8, 2026. The product lead may recommend
implementation cuts; changes to this outcome or its permissions need Mara's
approval. No such change has been adopted.

- Leah assigns at most eight offers to named existing standby members, in
  list order, one available seat per offer. No public signup, payment, or new
  account system. A decline leaves that chair empty for this workshop; there
  is no second offer round.
- Offers open October 16 at 18:00 and recipients have until 20:00 to accept
  or decline. An eligible acceptance saved before closing confirms that seat
  and puts the named member on the final admission roster. Merely receiving
  an email or submitting an uncommitted request does not confirm admission.
- Recipients may see only their own offer and saved status. Only the
  coordinator may assign or withdraw an offer before closing. Gate staff may
  see the final admission roster and record admission, but may not allocate
  seats or override offer decisions. The public must not receive the roster.
- A repeat submission or lost response must not allocate a second seat or
  conceal whether the first acceptance saved. A withdrawn or expired offer
  cannot subsequently confirm a seat. Closing must settle pending responses
  consistently before the roster is published. Accepted members must not be
  turned away because an earlier roster copy omitted them.
