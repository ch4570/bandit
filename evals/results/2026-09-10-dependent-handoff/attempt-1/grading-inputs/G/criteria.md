# Frozen independent criteria: dependent handoff

This rubric is separate from the task. Do not include it, this directory, the freeze metadata, or any evaluator analysis in a responding agent's context. Authoring used only the new synthetic task and repository policy, without viewing product skills, prior cases, outputs, rubrics, or issue/PR diagnoses. Evaluate meaning and practical consistency, not a required structure, exact wording, preferred offer, price, or duration. A justified defer/no-launch answer can earn full credit with a usable conditional offer and next action rather than a contradictory live advertisement.

Total: 100 points. Award partial credit for independently demonstrated elements. Do not award arithmetic points for merely naming a metric. Report criterion scores, total, critical findings, and supporting output excerpts. A total is not a validated product-quality benchmark. Any critical finding prevents a clean pass regardless of total; retain the numerical score rather than silently capping it. Normal clean-pass threshold: at least 80/100 and no critical finding.

## 1. Research-grounded decision — 15

- 6: Chooses a coherent customer/problem/offer using supplied observations and distinguishes observed needs, stated willingness, assumptions, and actual paid demand.
- 5: Gives contrary evidence decision weight: DIY rejection, D's unavailable bundle conditions, E's self-editing/tool gap, or F's low-touch preference materially affects scope, segmentation, offer alternatives, or uncertainty. Listing quotes without implications earns at most 2 here.
- 4: Makes a recommendation with one meaningful alternative or tradeoff. No compulsory number of options. Does not turn six convenience interviews, 11 emoji reactions, or a 32-person channel into proven market demand or conversion.

## 2. Feasible dependent customer/staff journey — 25

- 10: Sequences required customer inputs, staff production, customer review where relevant, revision/export, delivery, and usable downstream printing against their actual prerequisites. Each essential handoff has an intelligible owner and output. A review must reference an available preview; approval cannot precede production.
- 7: Separates 150 preparation minutes from 240 operating minutes and both 180-minute design windows. Dates accommodate the chosen service promise and the October 5 noon print-file deadline. Uses September 24 and/or 30 as warranted without pretending preparation creates client cards.
- 5: Gives workable handling for missing inputs, late/no feedback, corrections, and a full queue. Handles the consequence at the next dependent step, with customer-facing limits. No need to invent lengthy policy documents.
- 3: Every required customer step has an identifiable purpose. No extra approval rounds, meetings, or fixed-duration experiment imposed without customer value. A clearly disclosed no-review/draft-only offer can receive all journey points.

## 3. Offer boundaries and design handoff — 10

- 5: Clearly states buyer, contents, price or conditional price, inclusion/exclusion, correction/revision responsibility, and what the buyer must supply. Respects existing photo/template capabilities and distinguishes digital delivery from printing.
- 5: Designer can start from specified inputs, chosen deliverable format, production instructions, and acceptance checks. Relevant checks include content accuracy, readability, correct files, and consistent product facts. Does not mandate a visual style or a particular document template.

## 4. Usable UX and operating handoff — 15

- 7: Provides enough concrete screen/form/copy detail to prepare the existing tools: relevant product inputs, eligibility, package/price/expectation disclosure, submission/result state, and how to provide or omit review under the selected offer.
- 5: Connects customer-visible states to staff actions and records/notifications, including at least the failure states essential to the chosen flow. Text flow or tables suffice; no app build required.
- 3: Delivery guidance makes the result usable for the market, including customer-controlled printing and its deadline, or clearly explains the limitations of a draft-only deliverable.

## 5. Usable and truthful marketing — 10

- 6: Supplies publishable Korean recruitment or conditional interest-check copy with problem/value, concrete contents, price/condition, truthful date or deadline, eligibility/limit, and an actionable CTA. Copy matches the proposed purchase/application flow.
- 4: Uses the permitted channel and communication allowance sensibly. Does not promise higher sales, claim confirmed buyers, advertise excluded services, or hide a mandatory customer review burden. A defer answer labels interest collection as non-sale and sets honest expectations.

## 6. Business arithmetic and workload — 20

- 7: Calculates a realizable slot cap for the chosen offer using each design window separately and the operating-time budget. Includes fixed 48 minutes and all selected per-order work. Any reserve, changed workflow timing, or acquisition-overflow work is explicit.
- 7: Shows correct cash economics and meaningful volume sensitivity or break-even. Uses 3% on gross receipts and includes the full 130,000 won fixed cash cost unless clearly discussing a separate incremental decision. Zero paid demand is not treated as zero sunk expense.
- 6: Distinguishes cash result from owner-time result; separates first-time preparation from repeatable operations, values time at 20,000 won/hour, and states whether a chosen price/cap supports continuation. Avoids treating optional offerings or next month's availability as assured revenue/capacity.

## 7. Decision and learning closure — 5

- 3: Defines observable continuation/change/defer signals tied to the chosen uncertainty, with ownership and an appropriate decision point. Signals can include paid orders, completion, correction load, usability, or contribution; no mandatory list or numerical threshold.
- 2: Separates what this small test can reveal from unproven market or long-term business claims and identifies the next supply/demand assumption to check.

## Arithmetic reference checks (not preferred answers)

Use the following to check calculations when the corresponding offer is selected; do not require these packages or prices.

- Two-stage full service: first window requires 35n minutes; second requires (15+5)n = 20n; operating time is 48+(12+8+4)n = 48+24n. All first previews on September 24 and all finals on September 30 imply n <= min(floor(180/35), floor(180/20), floor((240-48)/24)) = min(5,9,8) = 5 before reserve. Six initial previews do not fit the first window even though their combined design time is below 360 minutes. A/B may review after receiving previews and by September 28 noon; others' review availability remains unconfirmed.
- One-stage, no-review final delivery: each order needs 35+5 = 40 designer minutes and 12+4 = 16 operating minutes. With indivisible orders inside a single window, four fit each window, so the cap is eight, not floor(360/40)=9. An explicit split of a ninth order across windows can be feasible only if its partial work and final export are correctly scheduled and the promised delivery matches; do not penalize that valid alternative. A draft/template offer may use a different justified export assumption.
- Mixed offers must allocate work to each date rather than count total hours alone. Extra service work reduces capacity; omission of a selected stage must be explained as a different offer, not silently erased from cost.
- With n identical paid orders at price P and no extra cash spending: receipts = nP; fees = 0.03nP; cash result = 0.97nP - 130,000. Cash break-even price = 130,000/(0.97n) for n > 0. No-order cash loss is 130,000 won.
- Full service n=5, P=40,000 is only a calculator probe: receipts 200,000; fees 6,000; cash profit 64,000; operating time 168 minutes costs 56,000; recurring owner-time result, assuming the same fixed cash cost, is 8,000. If all 150 prep minutes are consumed, first-run owner time is 318 minutes, cost 106,000, and first-run economic result is -42,000. At this n, cash break-even P is approximately 26,804.13 won; first-run full-preparation economic break-even P is approximately 48,659.79 won. Round up for whole-won break-even prices. If less preparation is planned, use that actual explicit plan rather than requiring all 150 minutes to be spent.
- Do not deduct design fees twice as per-order cost plus fixed-window cost. Dividing fixed cost across filled orders for reporting is acceptable. Do not mistake a quoted willingness range for a realized price or sale.

## Explicit critical findings

Mark a finding critical only when it affects the central recommended execution or a headline conclusion; report narrow arithmetic slips proportionately instead of inflating every issue.

1. Promises real customer card drafts/finals before the first available September 24 design window, or schedules required design work outside the two confirmed windows without explicitly conditional new capacity.
2. A required downstream commitment relies on a customer approval, supplied input, preview, or revision that cannot yet exist at the planned time, or ignores its absence while declaring the order completed. This does not force a review stage when none is sold.
3. The recommended accepted-order cap materially exceeds a confirmed per-window or owner-time budget under its own scope, without a disclosed feasible allocation, reserve adjustment, or conditional capacity change.
4. Converts stated interest/reaction/channel size into invented paid bookings or verified sales uplift and relies on that claim for the launch/BM conclusion.
5. The economics recommendation omits known fixed costs, counts unavailable labor as free cash-generating capacity, or makes an arithmetic error large enough to reverse the claimed viability, without disclosing an intentional incremental-cost comparison.
6. Publishes recruitment copy whose essential contents, customer obligations, price, or delivery promise contradict the recommended service enough that a customer would purchase a materially different offer.

Do not mark critical merely for choosing a different package, price, target segment, start date, total elapsed duration, number of customer interactions, or justified decision to defer. Do not retroactively alter this rubric to match an observed answer; any correction requires an explicit new version retaining this frozen artifact and the original result.
