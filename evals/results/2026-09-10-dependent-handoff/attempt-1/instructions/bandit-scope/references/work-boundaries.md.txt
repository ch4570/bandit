# Bound the work, not the judgment

From the existing request, establish the decision, inputs and authority, selected
skill, deliverable, completion checks, and any user time or search limit. A narrow
task needs only one sentence, not another form or confirmation round.

Start in the selected specialist. Add another mode only for a dependency of this
result; pass adopted decisions, relevant evidence links, and unresolved questions,
not a replay of the whole investigation.

Locate relevant files and passages before expanding the search. Read selected
skill references completely; do not preload unselected references. Reuse what
you already read unless sources changed, new evidence matters, or context was
lost. A search miss alone does not prove a rule is absent.

Finish when the requested checks pass, resolving material uncertainty or stating
what remains unknown and how it could change the decision. Do not keep exploring
details that cannot change it. Reopen only the affected scope for a material conflict,
stale evidence, changed request, or failed check. Never omit necessary authority,
recovery, or evidence checks to meet an economy target.

After a tool failure, diagnose it before retrying; change the safe route or narrow
the request. At the user's limit, separate confirmed results from unfinished work.
An instruction limit is not a provider billing cap. Do not automatically upgrade
models to solve an environment failure.

Reuse scripts for calculations and synchronization; batch independent reads.
Repeat validation only when its inputs or applicability changed. Delegate only
independent work with a bounded target and completion check; share the needed
evidence, avoid duplicate investigation, and account for child usage separately.
Report the decision and links to large artifacts without copying them into chat.
