Read together, this is a decision about continuing/revising recruitment, not permission to abandon one buyer.

Reserved availability does not prove all 280 minutes will be consumed. Actual checking/handling time must enter the economic review if it exceeds the assumption; this is not evidence of free labor or an arithmetic failure. Both short offers are already described as losses.
