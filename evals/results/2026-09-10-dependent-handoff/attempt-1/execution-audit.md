## Confirmed contradictions and conditional counterexample

1. **Case08 crosses the raw no-code boundary.** `request.md:13` says “Do not edit files, browse, contact anyone, or run code.” The completed JavaScript calculation at `tools:5–6` violates that explicit boundary even though it neither implements nor tests the product. `out:41` accurately claims a calculation tool was used but narrows the disclaimer to “No product code or tests were run”; the raw request did not contain that exception. The numerical result is correct; authorization compliance is a separate finding. No harness-level permission override was assessed.

2. **Case15's permitted late-input path cannot preserve its included follow-up.** `out:35` includes a short question after the answer. The normal order is Monday noon photo → Wednesday 22:00 answer → Friday noon customer question → Friday 22:00 staff answer (`out:46`). This provides 38 hours between the latest initial answer and question deadline. But `out:50` also admits photos until Wednesday noon, with an answer on Friday, saying the extra confirmation time is merely reduced. The confirmed staff windows are Monday/Wednesday/Friday 18:00–22:00 (`operations.md:8,11`). A Friday answer therefore arrives after Friday noon, leaving no opportunity to ask about that answer before the existing cutoff. No replacement customer cutoff and subsequent staffed reply are specified. The explicit final-week exception repairs this particular late-input branch only for the final week; ordinary weeks still contain it.

3. **Case15's “F is suitable” assessment conflicts with the response requirement.** `customer-notes.md:12` says F is available only on weekends and can read weekday replies later. `out:14` treats this as compatible. F can submit weekend photos before Monday noon, but reading Wednesday's answer on Saturday cannot lead to a question by Friday noon. In the final week the answer is November 4, questions close November 6 noon, and the last staff reply is November 6 evening (`out:48`); reading on November 7–8 cannot recover the included interaction. This is a source-backed customer-fit contradiction, not a forecast that all customers will miss the deadline.

4. **Case15's late first-week inputs admit a capacity counterexample.** The plan admits 24 customers and late photographs until Wednesday noon without a smaller late-input allowance (`out:3,50`). If all 24 use that allowed first-week deadline, Monday's photo-dependent processing window is unavailable. Wednesday plus Friday supply at most 480 raw minutes, even assigning the shared-record hour to Monday. The plan's own first-week workload is 24×(15+7)=528 minutes (`operations.md:18`; `out:56`), so completing the full promised first-week work within the remaining windows is 48 minutes short before additional rework. This is a conditional defect in the admitted path, not an observed arrival pattern or measured service failure. A cap on late entrants, earlier complete-input deadline, or explicitly revised service for late inputs would be needed; simply citing 660 weekly minutes does not resolve it.
