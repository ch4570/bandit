Adding the separately estimated weekly email would yield **5.5/5/2.5**, exceeding frontend and QA caps, so deferral is supported without assuming a second team or overlap (`B/input/delivery-constraints.md:3–17`; `B/output.md:29–44`). The 31 handoff and 17 ownership cases belong to the 62 late cases in the four-desk review, not the two-desk pilot; 116 consent records are out of 240 records, not consent inferred for everyone else (`B/input/evidence.md:6–20`; `B/output.md:11,42`).
- **D research:** Opportunities = `14 + 12 + 16 = 42`; memo-present opportunities = `11 + 5 + 12 = 28`; read-marked opportunities = `8 + 2 + 10 = 20`; assisted opportunities = `5 + 4 + 7 = 16`. `28/42 = 66.7%`, `20/42 = 47.6%`, and `20/28 = 71.4%`. These are opportunity counts, not numbers of individual notes, employees, or customers. Source definition: `D/input/research-records.md:22–30`; correct output: `D/output.md:19`.
- **D labor/cost units:** Support = `30/60 × 25,000 = 12,500` per store-month; onboarding = `40/60 × 25,000 = 16,666.67` once per new store. Five stores need **150 recurring support minutes** under the supplied assumption and **200 onboarding minutes**; the proposed four-hour separate onboarding reservation covers the latter. Sources: `D/input/costs-and-delivery.md:12–14`; `D/output.md:100,119`.
- **D model A:** `29,000 × 3.3% = 957`; recurring contribution = `29,000 − 957 − 2,000 − 12,500 = 13,543`. At five stores, `5 × 13,543 − 60,000 = 7,715` per recurring month. First cohort = `7,715 − 5 × 16,666.67 ≈ −75,618`. Fixed-cost recurring break-even is `ceil(60,000/13,543) = 5` stores. All agree with `D/output.md:104,107–109`.
- **D model B:** Monthly fee = `19,000 × 3.3% = 627`; recurring contribution = `19,000 − 627 − 2,000 − 12,500 = 3,873`. Setup fee net = `30,000 × 0.967 = 29,010`.
