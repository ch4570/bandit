# Supplied pilot report, 2026-09-07

August paid offer: 8 freelancers each bought one quote for KRW 9,000 and used
it. Two later received a full refund. The other six payments remained collected
at the reporting date. All eight had at least one later change-quote opportunity
during the 30-day window; two used the service for that later opportunity.

September preview: the offer was free and team draft sharing was introduced at
the same time. Recruitment also expanded from the founder's professional circle
to an open community. 24 freelancers joined. All 24 opened the app; 14 sent at
least one quote. Six of those 14 have had another quote opportunity so far, and
four used it again. The other eight senders have not had a second opportunity
within the elapsed 10 days. The other ten registrants have not sent a quote;
their opportunity status was not collected. No one was asked to pay during
this preview. No randomized assignment was used.

The preview's draft sharing was manual concierge coordination. Concurrent edit
conflict behavior in product software was not exercised. The product's v1 staging
test reports remain the only supplied execution evidence.
