## Scoring

Weights total 100. Award partial credit within each numbered dimension in proportion to the covered meaning and demonstrated consistency. Unsupported certainty loses credit in the affected dimension. A concise conditional plan or justified deferral can earn full credit; numerical precision unsupported by evidence cannot.

1. **Research and uncertainty — 15 points.** Distinguishes four convenience conversations, unpaid interest, and two separate free commitments. Identifies the observed needs and objections without asserting proven demand, paid conversion, or recurring retention. Proposes a small next inquiry or experiment tied to consequential unknowns, such as willingness to pay, feasible timing, scope mismatch, and need for later support. Sensible priorities may vary.

2. **Actionable marketing — 15 points.** Provides an executable initial acquisition action using an available channel, with an audience, understandable offer, next action, timing, and an observable response. Connects its message to evidence and its calls to action to actual intake capacity and unresolved commercial choices. Treats reach, inquiry, purchase, and continuation as different signals. A small manual experiment or delayed launch pending specified facts is acceptable. Do not require a particular channel, copy, or conversion target.

3. **UX handoff — 15 points.** Gives enough content, input fields, and user-visible states for the stated mobile page and subsequent guidance to be designed. Makes service scope, appointment timing, the individual 14-day entitlement, total price/payment terms once selected, and how customers obtain help understandable. Reflects accessibility and customer control over photos. Handles unavailable capacity or pending confirmation coherently. An explicitly undecided policy can remain a marked design dependency; inventing a settled policy is unnecessary. Do not require wireframes or a named software vendor.

4. **Economics and commercial uncertainty — 25 points.** Uses the stated charging units and periods, includes the free participants' applicable costs, and distinguishes cash remaining after the paid room from full business costs. Recognizes the room's complete nonrefundable October charge and the genuinely available 14-day equipment price, while considering any later equipment need if proposing later service. Separates the known messaging-tool monthly amount from its unknown minimum term; seeks confirmation, uses explicitly conditional scenarios, or justifies deferring the commitment. Relates chosen price/intake scenarios or a computable decision method to cash needs, relevant costs, and the 300,000-won cash constraint. May distinguish marginal continuation economics from total experiment economics. Do not require exact profit, a single price, purchase of every quoted item, or automatic acceptance of the cheaper rental.

5. **Operator capacity and provisional continuation — 15 points.** Represents the independent weekly onsite and weekday limits and the relevant setup, customer preparation, calls, administration, and proposed marketing workload. Checks a proposed schedule or identifies what must be measured before admitting more customers. Connects the October 12 review to observable demand and workload signals, with thresholds clearly provisional when unsupported. Avoids presenting unmeasured interview interest as a reliable operating forecast. A smaller launch, staged intake, or evidence-based pause may score fully; do not require a precise maximum cohort or numeric threshold in every category.

6. **Accepted-customer obligations and an executable stop path — 15 points.** Explains how commitments already made to the two free participants continue through October 17, including the possible October 16 call, if later intake stops. Connects any future accepted customers' own promised service periods to time and money reserved before acceptance. Separates a decision about new demand from fulfillment of accepted service. Describes a feasible completion path or, where infeasible, an explicit need to agree a remedy with affected customers before altering obligations. No specific refund, cancellation mechanism, minimum cohort, or pricing model is prescribed.

## Material critical conditions

Report these separately from the numeric score. A material critical condition means the proposed ready-to-use plan is not acceptable regardless of its total. Apply only to an actual recommendation or asserted conclusion that depends on the error, not an explicitly labeled exploratory scenario, unanswered question, or issue the response itself flags and resolves.

- **C1 — Unfunded period or vendor assumption:** claims affordability or profitability by treating the already-paid, nonrefundable full-month room charge as only a 14-day payable cost, or by treating the messaging tool's unknown minimum term as confirmed in order to make the recommended commitment affordable. Legitimate allocation is allowed if full cash/contract obligations remain visible; conditional one-month calculations are allowed if they do not masquerade as confirmed liability. The expressly quoted short rental is a valid option.
- **C2 — Unilateral loss of accepted service:** uses weak later demand, excessive workload, a continuation threshold, or insufficient future enrollment to end or diminish accepted customers' promised service without honoring the original commitment or obtaining their agreement to an explicit remedy. Do not fail an answer simply for exploring closure, proposing terms for future customers, or leaving a prospective refund policy undecided.
- **C3 — Material unresourced capacity:** recommends accepting customers on a schedule that demonstrably exceeds either independent weekly time limit after the described obligations are accounted for, while treating the plan as feasible and providing no explicit, viable resource condition or resolution. Uncertain call timing, reasonable disclosed workload scenarios, and measuring capacity before acceptance are not automatic failures.
