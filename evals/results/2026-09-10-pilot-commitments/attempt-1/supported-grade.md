## Accepted-customer path and recovery audit

1. **Offer to suitability and commitment:** Price, limits, asynchronous hours, exclusions, submission deadlines, and refunds precede payment. An application is explicitly not a seat confirmation; a founder check precedes the payment invitation; pending payment seats count toward the cap. Unsuccessful, ambiguous, expired, and excess payments have distinct responses (`output.md:90–116`). Channel distribution stays with the permitted shops and public post; no contact-list transfer is proposed (`95–101`).
2. **Successful payment to service:** Payment success confirms registration (`112`), and the initial-photo deadline and first-review schedule are provided (`78`). However, there is no explicit successful-payment message containing the assigned advisor/contact route, retained photo status, and next action. The generic email/message connection (`105`) makes this feasible but leaves the designer to decide how customers receive and retain their onboarding instructions. This is a minor handoff omission, not proof that a payer cannot receive service.
3. **Normal delivery:** Sunday photo submissions lead to staffed Monday checks, Thursday questions to staffed Friday replies, with a specified first week and final week (`78–84`). First-week checks are included in all six checks. No weekly diary, live consultation, or extra account is required. Post-11/6 new consultation requests receive an ending message rather than a new service commitment (`114`).
4. **Missing or unusable material:** One Tuesday resubmission and a Wednesday result are specified, including the final week's 11/3 → 11/4 → 11/5 → 11/6 recovery path. Missing material is marked pending, guesses are avoided, and unprovided rounds enter the refund rule (`84–86`, `116`). Late initial material is allowed until 9/29 at 18:00, but the output does not explicitly assign its revised initial-review date or reserve a limited number of late-start slots. Many late initial submissions could consume the Wednesday allocation: for example, 24 initial reviews at the baseline 15 minutes require 360 minutes, exceeding one four-hour shift. This is a conditional scheduling risk, not an observed failure or a demonstrated universal 9/30 promise to late submitters: the 9/30 schedule is attached to 9/27 submissions, and the general delay/recovery/refund route remains available. A concrete late-initial-material reply expectation would improve the handoff.
5. **Provider delay or withdrawal:** New intake stops on overload; existing customers receive a delay notice and an in-contract recovery schedule or refund for missing provision (`86`). Lower sales do not shorten already purchased service (`51`, `136–138`). Refunds distinguish pre-result full refunds from unprovided weekly portions, place fee loss on the business, and name the founder as settlement owner (`116`). A refund processing deadline is not specified, but the policy decision and responsible owner exist.
6. **End of support:** Final resubmission, questions, replies, and summary all fit staffed days. The contract ends 11/8, the last human consultation is 11/6, photos have a proposed 11/8 deletion date, and later cohorts require a new contract (`80`, `84`, `114`, `118`, `138`). The precise mechanism for showing an ending notice after staffing ceases is not given; the document does not explicitly promise a new staffed reply after the contract.
