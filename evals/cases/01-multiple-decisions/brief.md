# GatherTrip — current direction

The group typically has 4–8 people. An organizer creates a trip and shares its
join link. Each joined nickname appears on the participant list. A trip can have
more than one decision open at once: lodging and departure date are the first
two types. The host enters a few text options. Participants choose one option
per decision; a visible count is enough for the MVP. Responses can change until
that decision closes. The organizer makes the final choice, which is displayed
to the group. Opening the trip link alone does not add a participant.

No account system, payment, booking integration, chat, or reminders are in scope.
The team has not built the app. This direction was chosen by the founder; no
customer interviews or demand study are available.
