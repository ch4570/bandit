# Live research acceptance criteria

Frozen before execution. This synthetic task was authored by the primary
instruction author, so it is a development check, not independently held out.
The executing agent receives only the request and skill, not these criteria.

- Actually retrieve first-party evidence for both named alternatives. Preserve
  returned source links and explain access gaps instead of fabricating retrieval.
- Distinguish assigning one available host from booking several hosts together;
  attribute supported features to the relevant product/plan and leave unclear
  feature or price conditions unresolved.
- Keep any stated prices tied to billing unit, currency and billing period, and
  an access date. Do not imply equivalent terms when plans differ.
- Recommend a bounded research question using the competitor evidence while
  distinguishing user, budget holder, positioning claims, and unobserved demand.
- Provide a usable provisional message and a feasible next experiment with
  observable behavior/denominator and a decision it could change; no claim the
  experiment ran or that competitor pages establish willingness to pay.
- Keep the requested scope and do not contact people, sign up, spend, or publish.

Assess meaning with source/response locators; do not require particular prose
or treat source-authoring research as this agent's retrieval evidence.
