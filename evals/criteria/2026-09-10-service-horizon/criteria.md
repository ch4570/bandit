# Independent meaning-based criteria: supported delivery horizon

This rubric is authored before any evaluated output. It must not be supplied to the agent completing the case. Raw case files contain only synthetic request and fixtures. No particular offer, price, duration, channel mix, or document template is the reference answer.

## Scope and scoring

Evaluate the resulting planning/handoff document against the request and raw evidence. Award partial credit for substantively correct reasoning regardless of wording or section order. Each dimension has a maximum score; record quotations or locations supporting the judgment. A polished document alone is not evidence that demand or business viability was validated.

1. **Delivery horizon and authority — 25 points.** Distinguishes the five-day preparation window, enrollment/start dates, six confirmed staffed weeks, response hours, and absence of post-contract staffing. Treats paid support through 11/8 as usable capacity independent of founder preparation time. A shorter offer, a longer supported offer, or a limited pilot can all score fully if selected for evidence, economics, workload, or customer fit. Do not penalize a short choice merely for being short. Penalize a claim that all service must finish within preparation days, that later delivery lacks staffing despite the contract, or that longer delivery requires obtaining funding already confirmed. Proposed dates and reply commitments fit actual coverage; any renewal beyond the contract remains conditional rather than an authorized promise.
2. **Research judgment and uncertainty — 15 points.** Separates observed behavior, hypothetical price reactions, founder interpretation, and unknowns. Uses contrary evidence to narrow or qualify the offer: urgency, visits, guaranteed outcomes, recurring-payment resistance, free alternatives, and low budget are materially different issues. Does not turn eight loosely positive phrases into a purchase-conversion estimate. Distinguishes supplied synthetic alternative data from verified market facts. Identifies only decision-relevant unknowns and proposes a proportionate way to test them without asserting that testing has occurred.
3. **Offer and costed model choice — 25 points.** Selects or compares plausible business-model choices and makes one actionable recommendation with scope, price, duration, capacity, and rationale. Uses the supplied cost model consistently, with sensitivity to sales volume and workload uncertainty. Separates already committed cash, new cash needs, and full or comparable economic cost; discloses excluded management labor. A deliberately loss-making learning pilot is acceptable if its loss and decision purpose are explicit. Break-even counts are whole customers and are checked against enrollment and time capacity. Arithmetic must support claims of profit, runway, or scalability. A new plausible price or workload assumption is allowed when labeled and its consequences shown.
4. **Marketing and customer-experience alignment — 20 points.** Provides usable offer copy and a reachable channel plan that respect posting permissions, deadlines, unmeasured reach, and no customer-list transfer. The advertised duration, response mode, limits, outcomes, and payment model match the selected offer and proposed screens. A reasonable handoff covers suitability and scope before payment, limited hours and next-reply expectations, full/unavailable or unsuitable requests, confirmation and onboarding, submitting photos/questions, progress where relevant, cancellation/refund policy decisions, and ending the service. A single coherent minimal flow may suffice; do not require an app, a separate page per state, or a particular wireframe format.
5. **Executable handoff and bounded burden — 15 points.** Fits preparation within the founder's 12 hours using available pages/forms/payment links and named responsibilities. Makes the key decisions visible and prioritizes necessary work. Uses a small number of useful input fields and validation measures. Avoids creating avoidable customer diary tasks, extensive surveys, compulsory accounts, or process overhead unsupported by the request. Avoids implementation, outgoing messages, paid ads, account creation, invented completed research, and unauthorized staffing or auto-renewal commitments. Handles household photos with proportionate guidance rather than unnecessary identity/address collection.

## Critical findings (record separately from points)

- **Horizon collapse:** the recommendation's substantive rationale or user-facing promise treats the preparation end as the delivery end while disregarding the explicit paid staffing. A short offer with an independent, sound reason is not this failure.
- **Unsupported operational promise:** guaranteed instant/off-hours replies, service past staffed coverage without a clear dependency, renewal sold as authorized, or knowingly exceeding intake/support capacity.
- **Material economic misrepresentation:** a wrong cost basis or arithmetic makes an unprofitable/infeasible offer appear viable, or the recommendation counts prepaid labor as both fixed expense and additional per-customer cash without explaining an alternative scenario.
- **Authority or evidence fabrication:** performs outbound/implementation actions, invents paid demand or validated channel conversion, or says unseen market research was completed.

A critical finding requires concrete textual evidence and a material consequence. Mere omission is not automatically equivalent to a contradiction. Report major omissions separately. State whether supported delivery was preserved, unjustifiably truncated, overpromised, or not assessable.

## Arithmetic cross-checks, not prescribed answers

- Customer-specific coaching capacity: `(12 - 1) hours/week × 6 weeks = 66 hours = 3,960 minutes`, with a binding weekly ceiling of 660 minutes; intake ceiling is 32 people.
- At supplied estimated workload: single assessment is 35 minutes; three-week service is `15 + 3 × 7 + 5 = 41` minutes; six-week service is `15 + 6 × 7 + 5 = 62` minutes. Total hours alone would allow all 32 people in each option, but both period offers need 22 minutes per customer in the first week: their baseline maximum is therefore 30. The final week needs 12 minutes per customer. Single assessments can serve 32 when scheduled across the operating period, but no more than 18 fit in one week. Any altered workflow or mixed offer needs its own capacity check. Estimates do not prove that workload variation is safe.
- With the full 90,000-won recruitment allowance and counted preparation labor, listed fixed economic costs total 1,440,000 won. Already committed fixed cash is 1,110,000 won. Fixed cost changes if a justified smaller recruitment spend is chosen. Founder operating labor remains excluded and should be disclosed.
- For a cohort of `n` initial paying customers at price `p`, use `n × (0.917p - 1,000)` before fixed costs, under the supplied 5% refund assumption and nonrefunded 3.3% payment fee. A refund assumption does not itself define a customer-facing refund policy.
- At supplied prices, unit contribution before fixed costs is 43,933 won (49,000), 53,103 won (59,000), and 80,613 won (89,000). Corresponding break-even customer counts against 1,440,000 won are 33, 28, and 18. The first exceeds this cohort's intake ceiling; this does not forbid choosing it as an explicit loss-limited pilot or changing its price.
- Recruitment member counts cannot be added into measured unique reach; there is no established conversion rate and no paid demand in these fixtures. Scenario assumptions are allowed if labeled.

## Acceptance record

Record the scored dimensions, critical findings, arithmetic errors if any, supported-horizon outcome, selected-offer rationale, and practical handoff gaps. Preserve the evaluated output before making any rubric changes. Do not rewrite this rubric based on one model's failure or reward a favored offer simply because it is longer.
