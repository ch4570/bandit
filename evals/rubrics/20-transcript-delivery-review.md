## Design distinction and limits

This concerns a studio's audio-to-transcript delivery and source retention,
not quote approval, library notification reach, or onboarding investment. A
recording persists across provider attempts; provider output, stored artifacts,
editor export, quality acceptance, and source release have distinct meanings.
The linked records also carry different document, observation, approval, and
execution dates, including expressly missing dates. The review must preserve
supported history while preventing unsupported counts or recent document dates
from becoming release evidence.

The case does not require every ratio, rigid headings, a particular date layout,
or a predetermined feature priority. A positive process exit or matching output
shape is not a semantic grade. Any results are bounded development observations,
not superiority, causal instruction improvement, or general reliability claims.
