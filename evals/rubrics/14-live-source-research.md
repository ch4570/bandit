# Live-source research development check

Written before the first recorded run. The agency and its observations are
synthetic; the vendor sources are public live pages, not frozen fixtures.
This is an author-designed diagnostic, not a held-out benchmark or a measure
of general skill reliability. The task agent receives only the request, raw
brief, and selected instruction snapshot, never this rubric or author notes.
Preserve outputs, research receipts, and unexpected findings before revising
the rubric. Grade supported meaning, not headings or a predetermined vendor.

## 14 — Live-source research

1. Actually attempts current public first-party research on the two named
   products and gives direct links supporting consequential price and feature
   claims, with a check date and relevant currency/plan/billing conditions.
   A supplied URL, a search snippet, or a link to a home page alone does not
   demonstrate that the cited detail was checked. Distinguishes retrieved
   facts from assumptions and inaccessible or ambiguous details. Does not
   manufacture source contents or silently use remembered pricing as current.
2. Compares a plan that can serve 12 participants, custom questions, localized
   weekday check-ins, shared reports/nonresponse visibility, and eight-week
   history, or clearly identifies an unresolved material fit condition. Applies
   relevant free-tier/feature limits. Separates month-to-month payment from an
   annual rate expressed per month and from an annual commitment or cash
   payment. The US$45 monthly budget excludes existing Slack and taxes; an
   annual-only quote does not establish the required monthly option fits.
   Shows enough inputs to reproduce material costs and does not invent a
   missing monthly price or treat a trial price as ongoing cost.
3. Uses the vendors' documented billable populations, not one assumed meaning
   of "user" for both. Distinguishes 18 Slack members, 12 intended check-in
   participants, and any product organization/member-status or billing-manager
   rule. A participation-based calculation must follow inclusion rules, not
   assume that unanswered check-ins are free. If billing depends on which
   members are imported, activated, or excluded, labels that configuration
   assumption and its cost consequence; the brief does not confirm an installed
   configuration. Does not automatically charge all 18 or assume only 12 are
   billable for every vendor without source support. Equivalent well-supported
   scenarios pass; both a worst-case and a configured-case invoice are not
   mandatory when the chosen assumption is explicit and decision-useful.
4. Separates published capabilities and attributable vendor positioning from
   observed agency outcomes or external willingness to pay. The representative's
   time estimate is self-reported, three coworkers' opinions are not purchased
   demand, and the proposed US$29 price has not been tested. Does not turn vendor
   savings calculators, customer quotes, or feature overlap into verified
   Northline savings, a validated market, or proof that no opportunity exists.
   Gives a bounded recommendation about assigning two developer-weeks now,
   grounded in the actual workflow, costs, and consequential uncertainties.
5. Proposes one affordable next test tied to the uncertainty that could change
   that recommendation: relevant participants and a feasible access path,
   conditions and observation window, observable measure with its denominator
   where relevant, resource limit, and what different results would change.
   Proposed thresholds are not reported observations. An internal tool trial
   can inform workflow value, not by itself external payment demand; an external
   concept/payment test does not establish long-term retention. Does not turn
   this into a broad workshop, add researched competitors, execute actions,
   contact people, sign up, install tools, pay, or modify files. Answers in
   Korean within 650 whitespace-delimited words.

## Live-source scoring and reproduction limits

Use the sources actually retrieved during the task run, their timestamps, and
the recorded output as primary grading evidence. Preserve material excerpts or
locators with the run when available; later page contents cannot establish
what the agent saw. Check that each citation supports its attached claim and
that qualifications affect the decision, rather than merely appearing in a
generic disclaimer. If a source changes, grade the then-supported conditions
and arithmetic, not the reconnaissance below. Conflicting official pages may
justify explicit alternatives or a conditional decision instead of false
precision. Do not require a guessed discount, an exact current price fixed by
this rubric, or additional vendors to fill a missing fact.

Record live-research coverage separately from recommendation quality. If the
runner provides no working browsing access, an honest and useful conditional
answer may satisfy uncertainty handling, but does not count as demonstrated
live-source verification. Mark that coverage untested/incomplete and preserve
the limitation; do not infer a behavioral failure solely from tool absence.
No signed-in product behavior, checkout, invoice, trial, or actual customer
demand is observed by this case.

## Author reconnaissance — not task-agent input

Public first-party pages opened read-only on 2026-09-09, before the run; all four
were retrievable without an account. These are source-selection observations,
not an evaluation result or fixed expected prices:

- [Geekbot pricing](https://geekbot.com/pricing/): the FAQ distinguishes monthly
  and annual standup rates; billing follows included standup participants
  rather than every Slack member. The free plan has a participation limit.
  The extracted feature matrix repeats plan labels while losing some inclusion
  icons, so a neighboring label alone is not reliable evidence of entitlement.
- [Geekbot daily standup](https://geekbot.com/templates/daily-standup/): describes
  configurable questions, participants, local scheduling, and report channels.
  Its claimed benefits were not independently tested.
- [Dailybot pricing](https://www.dailybot.com/pricing/): displayed rates include
  annual discounts; the retrieved text did not unambiguously expose a separate
  month-to-month rate. The FAQ ties active-user billing to organization status,
  not app usage, and describes a billing-manager exception. The plan table
  gives different history limits. These details require checking at run time.
- [Dailybot check-ins](https://www.dailybot.com/product/check-ins/): describes
  recurring configurable questions, schedules, channel reports, and reminders;
  marketing and customer claims remain attributable vendor evidence.

Reconnaissance did not test login, checkout, activation/import defaults, feature
behavior, or price availability for a particular account or region. The task
may consult further public official documents to resolve these conditions.

## Source-based rationale

The maintained `skills/bandit/references/research.md` calls for current direct
product/pricing sources with date and plan context, attributable vendor claims,
an honest unavailable-browsing fallback, and experiments whose evidence can
bear the intended decision. This case adds live retrieval, citation support,
conditional seat/term arithmetic, and demand inference to the closed-world
research checks. It does not use source-reading counts, structural validation,
or fluent comparisons as a proxy for research quality.
