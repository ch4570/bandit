# Reachable-market development check

Written before the first recorded run. All businesses, source lists, money,
dates, and operating constraints are synthetic. This author-designed case is a
development probe, not a held-out benchmark or market evidence. Supply only
the raw request/brief and selected instruction snapshot to the task agent,
never this rubric or an author diagnosis. Record the rubric digest before the
run and preserve the complete output before grading or changing criteria.
Grade each criterion pass, partial, or fail by meaning rather than headings.

## 17 — Reachable market

1. Uses the buying/billing organization as the revenue unit, distinct from
   locations, daily users, and the cafes/restaurants placing orders. Reconciles
   the lists: 210 + 170 − 90 = 290 unique listed locations, represented by
   150 + 30 + 10 = 190 organizations (150×1 + 30×3 + 10×5 = 290 locations).
   Does not bill branches separately or treat recipient cafes as another pool
   of paying customers. The local supplied-list population is not evidence of
   the national or total market. Equivalent concise calculations pass; every
   intermediate formula need not be printed if the distinct units and counts
   are unambiguous.
2. Applies the actual proposed eligibility without upgrading descriptions into
   measured fit. The 50 explicit retail-only organizations and 30 non-producer
   resellers are outside the stated initial product. The 62 with external
   wholesale listed are a sensible potential first segment, but their weekly
   order situation and current tools are still unknown; the other 48 producers
   have an unknown sales channel. Thus at most 110 listed organizations are
   candidates for further qualification under the supplied categories, not
   110 proven eligible buyers, and 62 is not an established lower bound on full
   workflow eligibility. A narrower proposed segment may pass if its exclusion
   choices and remaining eligibility assumptions are explicit. Does not
   manufacture interview findings or treat missing sales-channel data as
   observed rejection or verified wholesale activity.
3. Grounds the reachable funnel in the founder's window and unique first
   contacts: at most 8×12 = 96 organizations before December's new-contact
   cutoff, limited also by the chosen candidate population. Repeated contacts,
   several branches, and the remaining December follow-up period do not add
   new prospects. Proposed response/demo/payment stages may make this smaller;
   exhausting the capacity is not already observed. At 60,000 KRW per active
   paying organization per month, 2,400,000 KRW MRR requires 40 organizations.
   For the full 96-contact ceiling, the target requires at least 40/96 ≈ 41.7%
   end-to-end contact-to-active-paid conversion even with every contacted
   organization eligible; qualification losses, slow starts or churn raise
   the necessary downstream success rate. A coherent narrower-funnel version
   may use its own denominator. Does not multiply weekly capacity by months,
   count the entire candidate pool as reached, or call quarterly receipts MRR.
4. Gives a useful conditional numeric range or multiple scenarios with explicit
   eligibility/adoption assumptions, applicable population, period, and price.
   Makes at least one consequential sensitivity or break-even condition visible
   so the founder can see when the target becomes plausible or implausible.
   The range need not use specified percentages, an elaborate funnel, or a
   particular table. May combine stages if the rate's denominator and meaning
   are clear. Does not apply an eligibility percentage twice, assume an
   unobserved list-wide conversion rate is measured, or silently change the
   agreed price/unit/channel to make the target work. Treats month-end active
   subscribers coherently: a no-churn/fast-onboarding simplification can be
   explicitly optimistic rather than an invented retained base. Briefly notes
   material unmeasured timing or delivery constraints; no full cost model is
   required. Directory counts and assumption-driven revenue are not evidence
   of demand, willingness to pay, retention, or profitable acquisition.
5. Makes a decision-relevant recommendation about committing development time
   now and identifies an economical next observation that could change it.
   Connects that observation to a consequential unknown such as actual weekly
   workflow eligibility, access to the buyer, response under the proposed
   offer, or paid commitment and time to adopt. Keeps its audience, channel,
   opportunity/window and effort consistent with the available founder time;
   proposed thresholds are hypotheses, not observed validation. No exact sample
   size, vendor, conversion percentage, or build/no-build verdict is mandated.
   A conditional proceed, narrower investment, or defer recommendation may pass
   when its arithmetic and assumptions support it. Answers in Korean within
   600 whitespace-delimited words as a short estimate and recommendation, not
   a market report. Does not browse, contact anyone, change files, implement,
   transact, or claim to have run the proposed observation.

## Source-based rationale and limits

`skills/bandit/references/research.md` says to size a market only when it informs
a decision, exposing reachable population, eligibility, conversion assumption,
unit, period, and price; distinguish the total market from customers this team
can reach; and show a range when plausible assumptions change the conclusion.
Its evidence guidance separates available observations, proposed thresholds,
payment, repeat behavior, and sustained commercial outcomes.

This case exercises those promises with overlapping location lists and
organization-level subscription revenue, not a trivia question about bakeries.
The arithmetic is internally constrained, but actual workflow eligibility,
sales performance, implementation feasibility, and buyer demand remain
unobserved. It cannot establish the service's commercial opportunity or the
general reliability of the skill. Do not reward an unsupported confident
forecast merely because it reproduces the reference arithmetic, or require
every possible funnel stage when a shorter defensible estimate answers the
founder's decision.
